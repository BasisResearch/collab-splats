Section 2: Startup — Session Selection

What does the user see first when they open the dashboard?

A

Modal overlay on launch

Dashboard opens with a blocking modal: "Start new session from video" or "Load existing results directory." Dismissed once a choice is made. App tabs are visible but disabled behind it.
